# Chapter 7: Free Goodwill

## Core Idea
A short interstitial chapter, not a framework chapter: Hormozi asks the reader to leave a book review, framed through his own principle that helping people with zero expectation of credit correlates with fulfillment, longevity, and even income. It doubles as a live demonstration of one of his own tactics — reducing the "effort & sacrifice" of the ask (under 60 seconds, step-by-step instructions per platform) to maximize compliance.

## Key Concepts
- **Goodwill transfer**: Hormozi's "life hack" — introducing something valuable to someone makes them associate that value with you; sharing the book itself becomes a goodwill-generating act for the reader, not just for Hormozi.

## Key Takeaways
1. This chapter is a worked demonstration, not a new framework: notice how the "ask" (leave a review) is deliberately shrunk in perceived effort and time delay — the same Value Equation logic from Ch6, applied to a call-to-action instead of a product.
2. Reducing friction on a low-stakes ask (specific platform instructions, sub-60-second framing) is itself a micro-example of Effort & Sacrifice reduction.

## Connects To
- **Ch6 (Value Equation)**: the effort-reduction tactics used in this very chapter's ask are the same mechanics taught there.
- **Ch8**: the book resumes its core teaching sequence with the Thought Process chapter.
