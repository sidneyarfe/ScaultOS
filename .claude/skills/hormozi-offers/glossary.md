# Glossary — $100M Offers

**Anti-Guarantee** — "All sales are final," justified by a credible vulnerability (e.g., the offer reveals easily-copied IP); filters for committed buyers on high-ticket or highly-exposed offers (Ch15).

**Archetype I / Archetype II** — Hormozi's client segmentation: Archetype I is under $3M/yr revenue (served with free content to build trust); Archetype II is $3M-$10M+/yr (candidates for his equity-investment model) (Ch2).

**Avatar** — the "A" in M-A-G-I-C naming; the specific, narrowly-defined ideal client named in an offer's headline (Ch16).

**Brick Exercise** — a 120-second divergent-thinking drill (list every use of a brick) used to train the idea-generation habit needed for offer-building (Ch8).

**Commoditized offer** — a product available from many places, purchased on price because it's perceived as equal to alternatives (Ch3).

**Conditional Guarantee** — a guarantee with terms tied to specific client actions, generally framed as "better than money back" (Ch15).

**Container word** — the "C" in M-A-G-I-C naming; signals the offer is a structured system/bundle, not a commodity (e.g., Blueprint, Bootcamp, Sprint) (Ch16).

**Convergent thinking** — problem-solving with known variables and one correct answer (e.g., arithmetic); contrasted with divergent thinking (Ch8).

**Cohort Cap** — a scarcity tactic limiting intake to X clients per class/cohort on a fixed cadence (Ch12).

**Delayed Second Payment Guarantee** — the client isn't billed again until they hit their first milestone/outcome (Ch15).

**Delicate Dance of Desire** — the principle that satisfying all demand kills future desire while satisfying none generates no revenue; profit lives in deliberately under-supplying relative to demand (Ch11).

**Differentiated offer / "Category of one"** — an offer positioned so it cannot be directly compared to competitors, enabling value-driven (not price-driven) purchase decisions (Ch2, Ch3).

**Divergent thinking** — problem-solving with multiple variables (known and unknown) and multiple valid answers of varying quality; the mode required for offer construction (Ch8).

**Dream Outcome** — the first driver of the Value Equation; the gap between a prospect's current reality and their envisioned future (Ch6).

**Effort & Sacrifice** — the fourth Value Equation driver (goal: decrease); the ancillary tangible/intangible costs required to get the result (Ch6).

**Exploding Opportunity** — an urgency mechanism where the underlying opportunity itself decays with time (e.g., arbitrage, early-mover advantage), independent of the seller (Ch13).

**First Outcome Guarantee** — the seller covers the client's ancillary costs (e.g., ad spend) until the client's first result is achieved (Ch15).

**Fractal (80/20) demand** — the observation that demand isn't linear; roughly a fifth of prospects will pay 5x+ what the rest will pay (Ch11).

**Goal** — the "G" in M-A-G-I-C naming; the prospect's dream outcome stated as a word or phrase (Ch16).

**Grand Slam Offer** — an offer combining an attractive promotion, unmatchable value proposition, premium price, and unbeatable guarantee, constructed so it cannot be compared to any competing product (Ch1, Ch2).

**Gross Profit** — revenue minus the direct cost of servicing one additional customer (distinct from net profit) (Ch3).

**Growth Rate Cap** — a scarcity tactic limiting intake to X new clients per week/period, communicated as live remaining openings (Ch12).

**Honest Scarcity** — publishing your real capacity limit and proximity to it, rather than an artificial cap; the most ethical and sustainable scarcity tactic (Ch12).

**Hormozi Law** — "The longer you delay the ask, the bigger the ask you can make" (Ch11).

**Implied Guarantee** — a performance/revshare/profit-share structure where payment is contingent on results by construction, functioning as a guarantee without explicit guarantee language (Ch15).

**Implicit-egotism effect** — people are drawn to things/people resembling themselves; the psychological basis for hyper-specific Avatar naming (Ch16).

**Interval** — the "I" in M-A-G-I-C naming; the stated duration/timeframe for results (Ch16).

**Lifetime Value (LTV) / LTGP** — gross profit accrued over a customer's entire relationship; Lifetime Gross Profit when emphasizing the profit (not revenue) basis (Ch3).

**M-A-G-I-C** — the five-component offer-naming formula: Magnetic reason why, Avatar, Goal, Interval, Container (Ch16).

**Magnetic reason why** — the "M" in M-A-G-I-C naming; the stated reason a promotion exists right now (Ch16).

**Monopoly profits** — the pricing power available to a business positioned so far above the market that direct comparison breaks down (Ch5).

**Niche slap** — Hormozi's term for entrepreneurs who blame the market and switch niches after one failed offer attempt instead of building a better offer for the same niche (Ch4).

**Normal market** — a market growing at roughly the marketplace's average rate with unmet needs in Health, Wealth, or Relationships; the baseline market condition the book assumes (Ch4).

**One-to-many solutions** — delivery vehicles built once and reused at near-zero marginal cost; the category producing the largest value-to-cost gap ("why software becomes so valuable") (Ch10).

**Perceived Likelihood of Achievement** — the second Value Equation driver (goal: increase); the prospect's confidence this specific purchase will get them the result (Ch6).

**Personal Service Guarantee** — the seller works 1-on-1 with the client, free, until a specific objective is reached, gated behind strict compliance conditions (Ch15).

**Price-to-value discrepancy** — the gap between perceived value received and price paid; the target metric for offer redesign — widen it by raising value, not cutting price (Ch5, Ch14).

**Sales-to-Fulfillment Continuum** — the inherent tradeoff between ease of selling (do more) and ease of fulfilling (do less); the goal is the sweet spot where both are true (Ch10).

**Scarcity** — a fixed (or perceived-fixed) supply that triggers fear of missing out; three types: Limited Supply of Seats/Slots, Limited Supply of Bonuses, Never Available Again (Ch12).

**Service Guarantee** — the seller keeps working for free until a result is achieved, removing time risk entirely (Ch15).

**Stacking** — layering multiple bonuses or multiple guarantee types onto one offer for compounding perceived-value effect (Ch14, Ch15).

**Starving Crowd** — a market with overwhelming, urgent demand; the highest-priority element in `Starving Crowd > Offer Strength > Persuasion Skills` (Ch4).

**TAM (Total Addressable Market)** — the ceiling on how large a business can grow within a given niche (Ch4).

**Time Delay** — the third Value Equation driver (goal: decrease); the gap between purchase and receiving the promised benefit (Ch6).

**Total Business Cap** — a scarcity tactic capping total ongoing clients at X, creating a waitlist (Ch12).

**Trim & Stack** — the fifth offer-construction step: trim delivery-vehicle candidates to high-value/low-cost and high-value/high-cost items, then bundle survivors into named, individually-valued components (Ch10).

**Unconditional Guarantee** — the strongest, simplest guarantee type; a pure "pay first, evaluate after" trial, highest conversion lift and highest refund exposure (Ch15).

**Value Equation** — `Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)`; the master framework for evaluating and building offer value (Ch6).

**Virtuous Cycle of Price** — the self-reinforcing chain triggered by raising prices (higher investment → higher perceived value → better results → better clients → bigger margin → reinvestment); reverses under a price cut (Ch5).
